## Code Of Conduct
Do Not Assume because we met before that you have permission to change or alter my software if you're not part of my open source team. Or change features without consulting me.

Do not use this software for purposes other than what it is specifically designed for: training NPCs and Enemies in Roguelike games.

Do not repackage and rebrand this software to attempt to compete with it and provide no attribution.

You must retain this code of conduct in its entirety, when forking to propose new changes. Said branches must meet specific minal operational standards: Unsolicited proposals are deleted unread.
